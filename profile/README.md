> We build CLI-first, local-first, agent-operable tools that give people more room to choose.

## Built for agency

Most attention is lost between intending and starting. We work in that gap: quietly handling repetition while keeping boundaries clear and decisions human.

- **Agency first** - AI should enlarge a person's choices, not reshape life around another tool.
- **Local by default** - Your work, models, and credentials stay where they belong whenever possible.
- **Useful to agents** - Every interface should work well for people and the AI agents that help them.
